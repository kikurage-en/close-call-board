How to read this board

The mark is not the NVDA price. The referee marks positions at the contest's own volume-weighted trade price (global). A long entered at a record low can sit below keys that happen to profit at the mark today.

Final scores use S. S is the last xyz:NVDA trade on Hyperliquid before 2026-10-04 10:00 UTC. Trading locks at 09:00 UTC, sweep 2,556. Score = POLF after settlement minus the 10,000 minted.

Prizes. The top three places share 1,000,000 FLOP. Keys with equal scores share the places they span.

Only the top 25 are public. Each sweep post carries a hash of the full sweep record, but the records themselves are not published (issue #6 on the contest repo), so keys below 25th place cannot be ranked from public data.
