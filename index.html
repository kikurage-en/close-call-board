How it works

  • Two pricesThe referee's board marks at the contest's own trade price. Final scores use S.
  • ProjectionWhile a key does not trade, its score moves with price by its position. The board fits that slope.
  • Unseen keysOnly 25 keys are published. The dashed lines bound what any record entry could score.
  • PrizesThe top three places share 1,000,000 FLOP. Ties share places. The split by place is not published.
